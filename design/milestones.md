### Accepted by design (stamped, not open work)

Kept because "we looked at this and chose to live with it" is a decision worth
not relitigating — unlike a to-do, which belongs on the board.

- Array-prefs last-writer-wins; `patchPrefs` rollback can clobber a concurrent
  fan-out (self-heals).
- RE2 has no explicit CPU budget beyond linear-time-by-construction.
- The M5 spec glosses (title-flash-only, away-only, log-location prose).
- A kicked/banned op lingers in the oplist until the next COL (matches live,
  cosmetic).
- PM dice rolls are swallowed — documented-channel-form-only is deliberate
  scope.
- A mid-stream export error yields a truncated 200 (inherent to streaming).
- `latest`/`edge` tag mutability (documented; pin `vX.Y`).
- Backups keep N+1 days (errs safe); the single-instance release worker is
  fine at VPS scale.
- Session-lifetime profile/insights client caches; sim IGN leniency.
- The eicon index postpones a failed refresh by a full window — deliberate,
  so a down host is not hammered.
- Name-charset drift across `NAME_RE`/`VALID_NAME`/`EICON_NAME`: three regexes
  serve two packages with deliberately different charsets, so consolidating
  risks subtle drift for zero behaviour change.
- A ≤2★ rating pick collapses the rated ad instantly, dropping the transient
  "Saved ✓" — the collapse *is* the feedback; ≥3★ keeps the flag.
- The M6/M10 supervised-live-pass confirmations, closed by soak (2026-08-02,
  user decision): two weeks of daily production use hit those areas without
  incident. Any untried variant surfaces as an ordinary bug report.
